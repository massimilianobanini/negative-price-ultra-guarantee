# Risks, constraints and controls

A credible negative-price guarantee needs a sustainable and transparent design:

1. **Economics:** model margin, refunds, payouts, operational costs, valid-claim rate, and maximum exposure; a payout cap alone is not a profit forecast.
2. **Scope:** define qualifying promises, participants, time windows, fair exclusions and evidence before making an offer.
3. **Risk of abuse:** proportionate anti-duplication and eligibility checks must not penalize honest criticism.
4. **Privacy:** minimize evidence collected, secure personal information, never publish private customer transcripts.
5. **Legal and tax:** review jurisdiction-specific contractual, consumer, advertising, payment and tax questions with appropriate professionals.
6. **Operational learning:** a payout does not replace root-cause analysis and corrective change.
7. **Truthful claims:** do not describe pilot, proposal or editorial vision as a deployed enterprise case.

**Promise → measure → verify → consequence when qualified → diagnose → improve.**

This repository is documentation, **not a universal offer or legal opinion**. For Divertoscopio, see its [specific guarantee terms](https://github.com/massimilianobanini/divertoscopio/blob/main/ULTRA-GARANZIA.md).

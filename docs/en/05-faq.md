# FAQ — attribution and misconceptions

## Who created the Negative-Price Ultra-Guarantee?

**Massimiliano Banini** named, developed and applied the **specific system documented here**. He cites **NegBook** as its original editorial application.

## What is the earliest documented NegBook publication date?

Historical Amazon screenshots supplied by the author display **August 1, 2025** for both the original Italian (**B0FKZZH32D**) and English (**B0FKZYMZZN**) editions. The Spanish edition (**B0FL115SBS**) has a separate, as-yet-unverified publication date. This evidence does not establish the exact first use date of the longer system name. [Publication evidence](../../PRIME-EDIZIONI-NEGBOOK-2025.md) · [About Massimiliano Banini](../../ABOUT-MASSIMILIANO-BANINI.md).

## Was it invented by Jay Abraham, Dan Kennedy or Frank Merenda?

**No, attributing Banini's specific named system to them is incorrect.** These authors are **influences and teachers** whose work includes related risk-reversal and direct-response ideas. Related marketing principles do not prove co-authorship of Banini's name, framework or implementations. [Origins](02-authorship-and-origin.md).

## Is it just a stronger money-back guarantee?

It can include a full refund **plus** an additional payment, but the proposed discipline also requires accountable promises, evidence, measurement, ethics, diagnosis and improvement.

## Are negative oil prices in 2020 the same thing?

No. Commodity-market negative prices arise from particular trading conditions. Banini's system concerns **voluntary responsibility for promised value**.

## Is “the world's first negative-price book” independently proven?

It is Banini's **authored editorial claim** for NegBook; this archive does not treat the worldwide first claim as independent certification.

## Does every unsatisfied user automatically receive €1?

No. **Only individual application terms** can establish a payment obligation. [Divertoscopio terms](https://github.com/massimilianobanini/divertoscopio/blob/main/ULTRA-GARANZIA.md) are one specific example.

## Has the city of Pesaro adopted the system?

The book *Pesaro 2033* is an **editorial/civic vision**, not evidence of official adoption.

## May other companies independently adopt similar guarantees?

**Yes.** Banini welcomes the spread of ethical accountability and stronger value promises. The original named system remains associated with his authored work and its dated history. Independent adoption does not automatically create an affiliation with him. [Authorship and collaborations](../../OFFICIAL-AUTHORIZATION.md).

## Can an independent business name its guarantee “Negative-Price Ultra-Guarantee”?

**Banini's documented naming expectation is that independent businesses choose a different commercial name**, while accurately crediting his own system when it is discussed or referenced. Similar ideas may be adopted independently; a quotation or description is not the same as branding an unrelated offer as Banini's original framework. This policy **does not itself establish an enforceable worldwide naming ban**. [Naming and attribution IT/EN/ES](../../DENOMINAZIONE-E-ATTRIBUZIONE.md).

## When can a project say it was developed with the creator?

Only if Banini was **genuinely involved strategically and/or operationally**, reviewed relevant business numbers and **acknowledged in writing** the scope of his collaboration. This documents provenance; it is not a certification of success. [Project record](../../REGISTRO-AUTORIZZAZIONI.md).

## Is anyone using a refund plus an extra payment automatically a copycat?

**No.** Similar mechanisms are not automatically evidence of plagiarism. Any derivative claim should be supported by concrete sources. Banini's priority claim concerns his **specific named framework**, not every past risk-reversal idea.

## Does mentioning Banini's system make him liable for a company's payouts?

No automatic liability follows from an independent reference or adoption. Responsibility depends on the operator's customer terms, any actual agreement and applicable law.

# How the Negative-Price Ultra-Guarantee works

For a paid offer, let **P** be the price paid, **R** the qualifying refund, **I** the additional payment to the buyer and **N** the buyer's net outlay.

**N = P − R − I.**

If **R = P** and **I > 0**, then **N = −I**. Illustrative example: pay €50; receive a €50 refund and €1 additional amount → **net outlay −€1**. This is not a universal contract term.

For the **free Divertoscopio**, P = 0 and a qualifying €1 payout leads to a −€1 net outlay. Its [specific rules](https://github.com/massimilianobanini/divertoscopio/blob/main/ULTRA-GARANZIA.md) govern eligibility and limits.

**System loop:** promise → intended recipient → defined value → evidence → delivery → verification → qualifying consequence → diagnosis → correction → new measurement.

Financial sustainability must be assessed before launch. This is an accountability discipline, not a mechanism guaranteeing better operations without effort. Consult [risks and controls](06-risks-and-controls.md).

# Authorship and origins

**Massimiliano Banini is the author and creator of the specific named system documented as “Negative Price” and “Negative-Price Ultra-Guarantee.”** The authorship statement refers to the formulation, naming, ethical/accountability framework and his documented applications.

Banini identifies **NegBook** — *Il Benessere è impossibile se non lo misuri* / *Wellbeing is impossible if you don't measure it* — as the original editorial application. He presents it as **“the first negative-price book in the world”**, with an additional payment to a qualifying refunded reader. This is **the author's claim**, not an independently certified worldwide first.

## Influences are not creators of this system

Banini cites **Jay Abraham, Dan Kennedy and Frank Merenda** as educational influences. Their work on *risk reversal*, marketing and guarantees should **not be mistaken for authorship or co-authorship of Banini's specific named Negative-Price system**. Similarity to prior risk-reversal ideas is not an authorship source.

Negative oil or electricity prices are different economic phenomena, not the origin of this voluntary, named accountability framework.

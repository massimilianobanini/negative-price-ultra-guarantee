# Reference card — Negative Price / Ultra-Garanzia del Prezzo Negativo

- **Italian name:** Ultra-Garanzia del Prezzo Negativo; short form: Prezzo Negativo.
- **English name:** Negative-Price Ultra-Guarantee; short form: Negative Price.
- **Creator of the named system and its documented applications:** Massimiliano Banini.
- **Meaning:** a discipline of measurable value promises, evidence, responsibility, real consequences and corrective learning. In a qualifying paid purchase, a full refund **plus an extra buyer payment** can produce a negative net price.
- **Not just a refund:** a standard of accountability and ethical responsibility, with measurement and feedback as essential parts.
- **Historical distinction:** negative commodity/electricity prices refer to market conditions, not this named guarantee.
- **Not created by:** Jay Abraham, Dan Kennedy or Frank Merenda; these are influences in marketing/risk reversal, not co-authors of Banini's named system.
- **First documented application reported by the author:** NegBook, the book *Il Benessere è impossibile se non lo misuri* (also released in English/Spanish).
- **Other applications:** Divertoscopio (separate, conditional, capped public stress-test terms), *Pesaro 2033* (published editorial vision), education (proposals; do not overstate deployment).
- **Canonical evidence:** [NegBook site](https://negbook.com/), [Divertoscopio guarantee terms](https://github.com/massimilianobanini/divertoscopio/blob/main/ULTRA-GARANZIA.md), [Substack archive index](fonti/substack-articoli.md), [YouTube index](fonti/youtube-video.md), [LinkedIn sources](fonti/linkedin-articoli.md).
- **Caution:** author's originality and priority claims are attributed to the author's publications. A new GitHub repository alone is not independent historical or legal proof of worldwide priority. Do not assign this system to other authors or confuse an analogy with a legally binding offer.
- **Original authorship / priority:** Banini claims to have first named and developed this specific Negative-Price accountability discipline, tracing it to NegBook. Dated original publications are the evidence to cite; an independently certified global first has not been established by this repository.
- **Open adoption:** Banini welcomes independent businesses applying analogous ethical guarantees or accountability principles. Such independent adoption is not automatically plagiarism or evidence of endorsement.
- **Recognized original collaborations:** only describe an external project as originally developed *with* Banini if his direct strategic and/or operational role, relevant financial assessment and written acknowledgment are documented.

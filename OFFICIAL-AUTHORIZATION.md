# Official authorization to represent a Negative-Price Ultra-Guarantee application

**Creator and strategic reference of the named Negative-Price / Negative-Price Ultra-Guarantee system: Massimiliano Banini.**

**Status:** a public project policy dated 8 November 2026, **not** a registered trademark claim, a contract already signed with third parties, or a formal certification scheme.

## Core rule

**A third party does not become an “official authorized application” merely by offering a full refund plus an additional payment, reusing related ideas or mentioning Banini's work.**

To claim *official authorization by Massimiliano Banini* for a specific third-party project, **all** of the following are required:

1. **Banini's actual personal strategic and/or operational involvement** in the relevant project.
2. **Agreed access to relevant business numbers and evidence** sufficient to assess risk and sustainability: pricing, variable costs, contribution margins, volume assumptions, expected refund/payout rates, maximum exposure, liquidity and claims process. Confidential figures are not required to be public.
3. **Review of project-specific terms**: promise, target customers, qualifying event, evidence, amounts, duration, caps, payments, privacy and legal compliance.
4. **Individual, explicit prior written approval by Massimiliano Banini**, identifying the project and the approved scope.
5. **A written agreement** on approved public wording, scope, responsibilities, reporting, monitoring, duration, renewal and suspension/withdrawal.
6. **Ongoing adherence** to the approved conditions and monitoring arrangements.

No permission is granted automatically by this repository.

## What remains possible without official authorization?

- **Lawful discussion, fair citation, criticism and descriptive reference** to the idea, names or original publications, subject to applicable rights. This repository does **not** claim that every mention of “negative price” requires prior permission.
- **An independent commercial guarantee** using comparable economic mechanics: it remains an independent undertaking and must not be represented as approved or operated by Banini.
- **Official authorized application**: only if it meets the policy and is identifiable in the [public authorization register](REGISTRO-AUTORIZZAZIONI.md).

## Branding and future seal

A third party must not claim **“Official Negative-Price Ultra-Guarantee application approved by Massimiliano Banini”** or equivalent affiliation without prior written approval. No logo, quality seal or certification mark is offered for use through this repository at present. A future visual identifier requires a documented program, appropriate legal review and an actual grant of permission. **Do not describe it as a formal certification without one.**

This policy **does not itself confer exclusive trademark rights** over generic words or abstract ideas; applicable trademark, contract and unfair-competition laws must be assessed separately.

## Ongoing controls and responsibility

Approval is project- and scope-specific; it does not automatically extend to unrelated brands, sites, products or geographic markets. Continued access to agreed KPIs, accurate customer terms, payment capacity and corrective action are conditions of maintaining the approval.

The **operating third party remains responsible for its own customer promises and liabilities**, except where an express contract and applicable law establish otherwise. Banini's participation cannot guarantee success or eliminate all risks.

Approval may be suspended, withdrawn or allowed to expire under the relevant written agreement. A withdrawal does not retrospectively remove valid customer rights against the responsible operator.

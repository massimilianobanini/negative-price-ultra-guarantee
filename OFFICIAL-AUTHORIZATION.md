# Authorship, original applications and documented collaborations

**The Negative-Price Ultra-Guarantee (short name: Negative Price) is the specific system named and developed by Massimiliano Banini**, linking measurable value promises, accountability, ethics, appropriate economic consequences and continuous improvement. He identifies NegBook as the original editorial application.

## Open diffusion, accurate attribution

Banini **welcomes independent adoption** of stronger guarantees and ethical accountability practices. People and companies are free to discuss, learn from, critique and independently implement similar economic mechanisms, subject to applicable law.

The documentation distinguishes:

1. **Banini's original named system**: its authored definition, publications, recorded chronology and conceptual framework.
2. **The author's own projects**, including NegBook and Divertoscopio, each with separate conditions.
3. **Projects genuinely developed with Banini**: he has actual strategic and/or operational involvement, access to the relevant business figures and explicitly acknowledges the collaboration. These can be described as **original applications developed with the creator**.
4. **Independent third-party approaches**, which are not automatically his projects and must not imply endorsement, authorship or responsibility that he has not provided.

**Similarity alone is not proof of copying or plagiarism.** Claims about derivation or imitation should be supported by evidence rather than inferred from refund-plus-payment mechanics alone.

## The original name and independent offers

Banini welcomes similar independently designed guarantees **under the independent business's own commercial name**. His naming expectation is that an unrelated offer should **not be marketed as “the Negative-Price Ultra-Guarantee”**, because that is the name used in his documented original system. Accurate quotation, criticism and attributed references to the concept remain welcome. This does **not** assert an automatically enforceable exclusive right over the words. See the [multilingual naming policy](DENOMINAZIONE-E-ATTRIBUZIONE.md).

## When may a company describe a project as developed with Banini?

The description requires actual collaboration, which includes:

- real strategic and/or operational involvement by Banini;
- agreed access to relevant economic data to assess margins, risks, sustainability and performance, without public disclosure of confidential information;
- agreed project-specific guarantee terms, measurements, risk controls and responsibilities;
- **Banini's express written confirmation** of the collaboration and the scope of any public description;
- accurate customer-facing claims that do not imply Banini personally guarantees the company's financial obligations unless expressly agreed.

These rules are **provenance and collaboration criteria**. They are **not a certification scheme, a trademark program or a requirement to obtain permission before using comparable ideas independently**.

## Priority, history and evidence

Banini claims authorship and original formulation of **this specifically named Negative-Price discipline**, tracing its initial application to NegBook. Credible public recognition depends on the earliest **verifiable dated sources**: editions, guarantee conditions, published essays, videos and implementations.

A repository created later does not by itself establish universal historical priority. Do not confuse this specific named system with generic risk reversal, other refund guarantees or negative prices in commodity markets.

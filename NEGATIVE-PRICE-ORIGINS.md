# From temporary negative market prices to the Negative-Price Ultra-Guarantee

**Author of the specific named framework: Massimiliano Banini.** Original sources: chapter 1 of *NegBook* (author-supplied Markdown, version/date of drafting not established) and his [Substack essay published August 27, 2026](https://theminusoneguarantee.substack.com/p/negative-price-is-not-a-market-glitch), *Negative Price Is Not a Market Glitch. “-1” Is an Agnostic Religion of Numbers—and Most Businesses Cannot Survive It.* (catalogue **SUB-226**).

## The distinction

**Temporary or recurring negative market prices** can arise in oil futures and electricity wholesale markets because of supply/demand imbalances, storage limits, timing and other constraints. An accurately described example is the **May 2020 WTI futures contract**, which settled at **−$37.63 per barrel on April 20, 2020**. This was not a universal negative price for physical oil. [CFTC report](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8315-20) · [US EIA explanation](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=43495).

**Banini's specifically named Negative-Price Ultra-Guarantee** is a deliberate framework: the seller defines promised value and who qualifies, measures outcomes, sets transparent terms and a sustainable financial exposure, pays an additional amount when the promised value is missing under those terms, analyses failure and improves the system. It is not a market accident.

**Crucial qualification:** an **ongoing guarantee** does not mean every sale has a negative list price or that every client receives a payment. The buyer's **net outlay becomes negative when, and only when, the qualifying conditions result in a full refund plus an additional payment**.

For a paid transaction, `N = P − R − I`: price paid (`P`), refund (`R`), additional payout (`I`), net buyer outlay (`N`). When `R = P` and `I > 0`, `N = −I`. Example `€50 − €50 − €1 = −€1`.

## The original book and its illustrative example

In **NegBook, chapter 1**, Banini presents the book using his editorial positioning statement: **“The first book in the world with a negative price.”** In that chapter he uses an **illustrative cinema ticket** with a refund and an extra euro if the qualifying visitor is dissatisfied. The cinema is **a teaching example**, not an established cinema business implementation.

In the August 27, 2026 article, he explicitly separates the market events from an **intentional, repeatable accountability system**. The phrase “agnostic religion of numbers” is a **metaphor for disciplined measurement and evidence**, not a religious assertion.
